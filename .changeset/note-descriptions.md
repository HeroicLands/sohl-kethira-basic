**Every skill, mystical ability, birthsign, faith tradition and arcane
convocation now carries a one-sentence description.** 342 notes that
previously showed nothing in a compendium listing or search result now state
what they are — an arcane incantation names the convocation it belongs to, a
skill names its kind, a birthsign and a faith tradition name themselves.

**Bandits** — Bandits 2 through 5 and the first bandit archer carry the same appearance and dossier details their fellows do, so a referee reading any of them finds age, build, colouring, trade and birthplace in the same place.

**Birth dates** — A bandit's recorded birth date is the date they were born, in the year they were born. Chéleb al Rhýddyn's is read as the day it states.

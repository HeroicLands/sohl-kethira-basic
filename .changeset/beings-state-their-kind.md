**Characters and creatures** — Every being says what it is. The pregenerated characters and the HMK Basic Folk template are characters, the bandits are NPCs, and the gárgun and ivâshu are creatures, so each page names its kind.

**Archetypes** — A character or NPC page lists the archetypes it fits, drawn from the being's own trade and skills: the bandits skirmish, their leaders are warriors, Elýsè heals under a guild, Chéleb reads wild country, Tórnis gets in unseen and trades on the way out.

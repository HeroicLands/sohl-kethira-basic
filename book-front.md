# HârnMaster Kethira Basic

The basic mechanical pieces for playing Song of Heroic Lands in the world of
Kethíra. This volume carries the sample beings a referee can run as they stand;
the skills, mystical abilities, sunsigns and traditions the module ships are
mechanical entries, and they live in the compendiums rather than on a page.

HârnMaster Kethira itself is what explains them, and it is bought from Keléstia
Productions.
